# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

**Business Insights 360** is an interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard designed to provide a **360° view of business performance** across finance, sales, marketing, supply chain, and market share.

The dashboard transforms business data into interactive visual insights that help users monitor KPIs, identify trends, analyze performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall business performance
* Analyze sales and profitability
* Track financial KPIs and P&L performance
* Evaluate supply chain and operational performance
* Analyze marketing performance
* Understand market share and business trends
* Provide an executive-level overview of key metrics
* Enable interactive filtering and drill-down analysis

---

## 📑 Dashboard Views

### 🏠 Home

Provides navigation to the different sections of the dashboard and acts as the main landing page.

### 👔 Executive View

Provides a high-level overview of important business KPIs and performance indicators for management-level analysis.

### 💰 Financial View

Focuses on financial performance, profitability, expenses, and other key financial metrics.

### 🚚 Supply Chain View

Analyzes supply chain and operational performance, including relevant cost and efficiency indicators.

### 🛒 Sales View

Provides detailed analysis of sales performance across products, markets, customers, and time periods.

### 📢 Marketing View

Analyzes marketing-related performance and business contribution using interactive KPIs and visualizations.

### 📈 Market Share

Provides insights into market position and market-share-related metrics across different business dimensions.

### ℹ️ Info & Support

Contains supporting information and guidance for navigating and understanding the dashboard.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbit` Power BI template.
2. Open the file using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Connect or provide the required data sources if prompted.
4. Refresh the data.
5. Navigate through the dashboard using the available pages and filters.
6. Use slicers and interactive visuals to explore business performance.

## 💡 Business Value

Business Insights 360 brings multiple business functions into a single analytical environment, allowing users to move from **high-level executive insights to detailed operational analysis**.

It demonstrates how Power BI, DAX, data modeling, and visualization can be combined to create an interactive business intelligence solution.
